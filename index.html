01 Problem Snapshot
Trust Deficit
No SEBI badge, AUM, or social proof above the fold. First-timers bounce before reading.
KYC Friction
No progress bar, no save-and-resume. Users hit a wall and don't come back.
Decision Paralysis
15+ deals, zero curation. No "start here" signal for new investors.
Zero Activation Hook
Post-KYC = blank dashboard. No guided path, no nudge to first investment.

03 Top 3 Drop-off Insights
Landing → Signup
−65%
  • Value prop is vague — "alternative investments" means nothing
  • No trust signals above fold (AUM, SEBI, investor count)
  • Psychology: unfamiliarity triggers exit before scroll
Signup → KYC
−60%
  • No progress indicator — effort feels open-ended
  • No save-and-resume; one failed upload = restart
  • Psychology: perceived effort far exceeds perceived reward
Browse → Invest
−56%
  • 15+ deals, no curation → choice overload
  • Jargon (yield, tenor, coupon) not explained inline
  • Psychology: decision paralysis kills intent
04 Recommendations
⚡ Quick Wins — Ship in Sprint 1
  • Rewrite landing headline → outcome-led: "Earn 11–13% p.a. with Secured Bonds"
  • Add social proof strip → "₹500Cr+ invested · 50K investors · SEBI registered" above the fold
  • KYC progress bar → "Step 2 of 4 · ~3 mins left" + save-and-resume state
🚀 High-Impact — Sprint 2–3
  • "First Investment Picks" → default deals view for new users (3 curated deals, low min)
  • Returns simulator → "Invest ₹25K → earn ₹X by [date]" — inline on deal cards
05 Final Insight
Core Problem
Grip Invest loses 96% of visitors before a rupee is invested — not because the product is weak, but because the journey to trust it is broken.
Biggest Opportunity
Fix KYC friction + add a curated first-investment flow. KYC 40%→62% and browse-to-invest 44%→60% means overall conversion more than doubles — without spending a rupee more on acquisition.
